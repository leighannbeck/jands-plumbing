It's all in the family

J&S Plumbing and Heating has been serving the community since 1981, when Jerry Beck acquired Lane Plumbing Company and transformed it into a thriving family business. Under Jerry's leadership as owner, the company expanded its expertise to include a full-service HVAC department. In 2018, Matthew Beck continued the family tradition by purchasing the business from his father and establishing J&S as an LLC, ensuring the same commitment to quality service for generations to come.

Our Services

Designed for business teams like yours

Here at Flowbite we focus on markets where technology, innovation, and capital can unlock long-term value and drive economic growth.

Maintenance and Repair

We provide comprehensive maintenance and repair services for all your plumbing needs, including fixtures, water heaters, and interior leak detection. Our HVAC services cover heating systems, air conditioners, and evaporative coolers, ensuring year-round comfort for your home or business.

Water and Sewage

Our experienced technicians locate and repair leaks in water and sewer lines, clear sewage stoppages, and handle complete installation of new water and sewer systems. We deliver reliable solutions to keep your property's infrastructure running smoothly.

HVAC Seasonal Changes

We shut down your heating system and perform complete startup services for your cooling system, whether evaporative coolers or air conditioning units.

We properly shut down cooling systems with preventative maintenance to avoid freezing and leaks, then start up and service your heating system, including thorough cleaning and filter replacement.

Custom Duct Fabrication

We specialize in custom-designed ductwork for new installations in both residential and commercial properties. Our sheet metal fabrication services are also available for walk-in customers with specialized needs.

Gas & Liquid Propane

We conduct gas pressure testing for residential, commercial, and mobile home properties, and provide expert leak detection and repair services to ensure your safety and peace of mind.

Commercial HVAC

Our team services and repairs commercial HVAC units of all sizes. Contact us for detailed estimates on new commercial projects tailored to your business needs.
